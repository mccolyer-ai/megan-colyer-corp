Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

This policy explains how Megan Colyer Corp ("we," "us") collects and uses information through our wholesaler portal, buyer portal, and deal-submission pages.

What We Collect

  • Contact information you give us: name, email address, and phone number.
  • Property and deal details you submit: address, price, condition, photos, and notes.
  • Buy-box preferences you submit as a buyer: price range, locations, and property criteria.
  • If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from your Google account to verify your identity — we do not receive your Google password.

How We Use It

  • To log you into your wholesaler or buyer portal account.
  • To match you with relevant off-market deals or buyers.
  • To follow up with you about deals, offers, and partnership opportunities.
  • To send you transactional emails, such as login codes.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share deal and contact details only as needed to close transactions (for example, with a buyer interested in a property you submitted, or a wholesaler whose deal matches your buy box).

Your Choices

You can ask us to update or remove your information at any time by contacting us at 636-698-1412 or through the contact details on our site.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at 636-698-1412.
